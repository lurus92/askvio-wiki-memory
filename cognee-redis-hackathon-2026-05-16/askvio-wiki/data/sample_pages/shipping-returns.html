Tracking

A tracking number is emailed within 1 business day of dispatch. Track your order on the carrier's website or in your UrbanWear account under "My Orders". USPS is used for Standard domestic; UPS for Express and Overnight; DHL for international.

Lost or Damaged Packages

If your package is lost or arrives damaged, contact support@urbanwear.com within 14 days of the expected delivery date. Include your order number and photos of any damage. We will replace or refund at no cost to you.

Returns & Exchanges

Return Policy

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for unworn, unwashed items in original packaging with all tags attached. Sale items marked "Final Sale" are non-returnable.

How to Start a Return

  1. Visit urbanwear.com/returns and enter your order number and email address.
  2. Select the items you wish to return and the reason.
  3. Print the prepaid USPS return label (US customers only). International returns: ship at your own cost to our Columbus warehouse.
  4. Drop off the package at any USPS location within 7 days of generating the label.
  5. Once received and inspected (2–3 business days), a refund is issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item and place a new order for the replacement. If you used a discount code on the original order, contact support and we will apply it to your new order.

Refund Policy

  • Refunds go to the original payment method only.
  • Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (wrong item, defective product).
  • Return shipping costs are deducted from the refund for US customers ($6.95 for the prepaid label). International customers bear their own return shipping cost.
  • Gift cards: refunded as store credit.
  • Refunds typically appear in 5–7 business days after processing; PayPal refunds may appear faster.

Defective or Incorrect Items

If you received the wrong item or a defective product, we will cover all return shipping costs and either replace the item or issue a full refund, including original shipping. Email support@urbanwear.com with your order number and a photo.

Order Changes & Cancellations

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement by contacting our support team at 1-800-555-0192 or support@urbanwear.com. After 1 hour, orders enter the fulfilment queue and cannot be changed.

If you need to change a size or colour after the 1-hour window, wait for the order to arrive and initiate a return.

Gift Orders

Select "This is a gift" at checkout to exclude price information from the packing slip. Gift receipts can be included on request. The recipient can use our returns portal with the gift receipt code to return or exchange.
