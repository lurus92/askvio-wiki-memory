Frequently Asked Questions

Ordering & Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay, and UrbanWear gift cards. We do not accept bank transfers, cheques, or cryptocurrency.

Can I use multiple discount codes on one order?

Only one discount code can be applied per order. Discount codes cannot be combined with each other, but they can be used alongside a gift card.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. All transactions are processed through Stripe, which is PCI DSS Level 1 certified. UrbanWear never stores your full card number. All pages are served over HTTPS with TLS 1.3.

Do you offer payment plans or Buy Now Pay Later?

Yes, we offer Shop Pay Installments (powered by Affirm) for orders between $50 and $3,000. You can split into 4 interest-free bi-weekly payments or 6–24 month plans. Select "Shop Pay" at checkout and choose the instalment option.

Can I change my order after placing it?

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of placement. After that, changes are not possible. Contact support immediately at 1-800-555-0192 (Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm ET).

Do you offer student or military discounts?

Yes. Students can verify through UNiDAYS for 15% off. Military and first responders can verify through ID.me for 20% off. These discounts apply to full-price items only and cannot be combined with other codes.

Products & Sizing

How do I find the right size?

Each product page has a size guide with measurements in inches and centimetres. We recommend measuring your chest, waist, and hips and comparing against the size chart. When in doubt, size up — our items run slightly slim. Still unsure? Email fit@urbanwear.com with your measurements and the product SKU.

Are your clothes true to size?

Most items run slightly slim. Product pages include customer fit feedback (e.g. "87% say this fits true to size"). Check that section for the specific item you're considering.

Do you restock sold-out sizes?

Core styles are restocked regularly, usually within 4–6 weeks. Limited-edition and seasonal items may not be restocked. Use the "Notify me when back in stock" button on the product page to receive an email alert.

What does "Final Sale" mean?

Final Sale items are marked down by 50% or more and cannot be returned or exchanged. All sales are final. This is indicated clearly on the product page and at checkout.

Are your materials sustainable?

80% of our fabrics are certified by OEKO-TEX Standard 100, meaning they are tested for harmful substances. We use recycled polyester where possible and are working toward 100% sustainable sourcing by 2028. Our denim line uses 60% less water in production than conventional denim.

Account & Loyalty

Do I need an account to order?

No, you can check out as a guest. However, creating an account lets you track orders, save addresses, earn loyalty points, and access your full order history.

How does the loyalty programme work?

Earn 1 point for every $1 spent. 100 points = $5 off a future order. Points are credited after an order is delivered and the return window has passed (30 days). Points expire after 24 months of account inactivity. You can check your balance at urbanwear.com/account/loyalty.

Can I transfer loyalty points?

No, points are non-transferable and have no cash value.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Click "Forgot password" on the login page. You'll receive a reset email within 2 minutes. Check your spam folder if you don't see it. If you continue to have trouble, contact support@urbanwear.com.

Contact & Support

How can I reach customer support?

  • Email: support@urbanwear.com (response within 24 hours on business days)
  • Phone: 1-800-555-0192 (Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm ET)
  • Live chat: Available on the website Mon–Fri, 9am–9pm ET and Sat–Sun, 10am–6pm ET

Where is UrbanWear based?

Our headquarters and main warehouse are in Columbus, Ohio, USA. We ship from Columbus, and all customer support is handled in-house from Columbus.

Do you have physical stores?

Yes — we have flagship stores in New York (SoHo), Los Angeles (West Hollywood), and Chicago (Wicker Park). Store hours vary; check urbanwear.com/stores for current hours and addresses.

How do I leave a product review?

After your order is delivered, you'll receive an email inviting you to leave a review. You can also go to the product page and click "Write a review." You must have purchased the item to leave a verified review.

Gift Cards

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Digital gift cards are available in denominations of $25, $50, $100, $200, and $500. They are delivered by email immediately after purchase and never expire. Physical gift cards are available in-store only. Gift cards cannot be used to purchase other gift cards.
